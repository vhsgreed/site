## Policy notes

- **Why log rumors at all:** supply chains leak on social media months before
  filings confirm. A dated rumor log lets us (and buyers) check our
  calibration later: which weak signals resolved true?
- **What disqualifies a claim from ever entering the dataset:** anonymous
  documents (#5), engagement-bait (#6), or anything without a named human or
  outlet attached.
- **The one to watch:** #8 (MPWR allocation). It has a named shop, a stock
  reaction, and a concrete falsifiable consequence. If Edgewater is right,
  the power tier of the main map changes.
