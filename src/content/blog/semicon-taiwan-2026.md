SEMICON Taiwan is the semiconductor industry's biggest annual gathering in Asia, and the 2026 edition is a record-scale one: over 1,200 exhibitors across TaiNEX Halls 1 and 2 in Taipei, September 2-4. Here is what it is, what happens there, and how to get value from it even if you never board a plane.

## The facts

| Fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Dates (exhibition floor)** | September 2-4, 2026 (Wednesday to Friday) |
| **Venue** | Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center (TaiNEX), Halls 1 and 2, Taipei, Taiwan |
| **Conference forums** | August 31 to September 4, 2026 |
| **Organizer** | SEMI (SEMI Global) |
| **Scale (2026 edition)** | Around 1,200-1,300+ exhibitors and 4,100-4,300 booths, reported as record scale in the event's 30-year history |
| **Access** | Taipei Metro, Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center station (Bannan Blue Line / Wenhu Brown Line) |

Sources: the official SEMI event page (expo.semi.org) and trade-press coverage, checked 2026-08-31. Attendance figures are organizer-reported expectations and can shift; always confirm on the official site before booking.

## Why this show matters

SEMICON Taiwan sits at the center of gravity of advanced chip manufacturing: Taiwan fabs the world's leading-edge silicon, and SEMI's show is where equipment makers, materials suppliers, packaging houses, and foundries all exhibit in one place. The 2026 edition spotlights themes including advanced manufacturing, heterogeneous integration and chiplets, compound semiconductors, automotive chips, smart manufacturing, and semiconductor cybersecurity. The Smart Fab zone covers the automation-to-autonomous-production transition, including collaborative and humanoid robots and automated material handling: the point where semiconductors and robotics supply chains physically meet.

The exhibitor list is public before the show, and it is a research asset on its own: a map of who considers this market important enough to buy floor space.
