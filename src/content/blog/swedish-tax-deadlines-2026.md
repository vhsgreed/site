Swedish filing deadlines are simple once you see the whole year on one
page. This is that page for a sole trader (enskild firma), with the source
for every date. It is not tax advice; confirm current rules on
[skatteverket.se](https://www.skatteverket.se) before filing.

## The one that matters most: 4 May 2026

The main income tax return for individuals and sole traders,
**Inkomstdeklaration 1**, is due **4 May 2026**. If you run an enskild
firma, your business result goes into that return on the **NE-bilaga**
attachment. Skatteverket's own page for enskild näringsverksamhet states
the date plainly: *"Sista dagen för att deklarera är den 4 maj 2026."*

- **4 May 2026**: last day to file Inkomstdeklaration 1 (electronically)
- **1 June 2026**: last day if you have anstånd (extension). You get anstånd
  by asking Skatteverket in advance; do not assume it is automatic.
- Paper forms have earlier dates (typically mid-April); e-filing is the
  default route and the later deadline.

## Moms (VAT) during the year

Moms deadlines depend on your reporting period, which Skatteverket assigns
based on your turnover:

- **Quarterly** (most small firms start here): due on the 12th of the second
  month after each quarter ends. Q4 2025 moms was due 12 February 2026;
  Q1 2026 on 12 May 2026; Q2 on 12 August 2026; Q3 on 12 November 2026.
- **Monthly**: the 12th of the following month.
- **Annual**: possible once turnover is low enough; the return follows the
  income year.

Register your reporting period when you register for moms. Changing it
later requires applying to Skatteverket.

## Other dates worth having in the calendar

- **Preliminary income tax (preliminär skatt)**: paid on the schedule
  Skatteverket issues (usually 12 payments per year). If your profit changes
  a lot, apply for adjustment rather than waiting for the final statement.
- **Employer contributions (arbetsgivaravgifter)**: only relevant if you
  hire; due with the monthly/periodic reporting.
- **Bookkeeping closure**: close the books before filing. The
  [one-pool bookkeeping method](/blog/enskild-firma-accounting-sweden/) for
  a solo digital firm exists precisely so that this week is not painful.

## The May checklist for an enskild firma

1. Close the books for the income year (revenue, costs, result).
2. Fill in NE-bilaga: revenue, expenses, result, and any special lines that
   apply (check the current NE-bilaga instructions; the form number changes
   between years).
3. File Inkomstdeklaration 1 with the NE attachment, electronically via
   Skatteverket's e-service or file transfer.
4. Note the besked om slutlig skatt (final tax statement) when it arrives,
   and check the preliminary schedule it implies.
