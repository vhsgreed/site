Skatteverket's catalog runs to thousands of numbered forms, and the official
index is entirely in Swedish. For a solo founder, the good news is that a
one-person firm touches only a handful of them. This is the map, in the
order you will meet them. It is not tax advice; the authoritative versions
live at [skatteverket.se](https://www.skatteverket.se) and
[verksamt.se](https://verksamt.se).

## 1. Business registration (before any form)

Registering an enskild firma happens at [verksamt.se](https://verksamt.se),
the joint government e-service, not on a paper form. The registration flow
includes the F-skatt application. The paper alternative exists but is slow;
the digital route is the default for a reason.

## 2. F-skatt / FA-skatt application

The application for tax approval is evaluated on your business substance:
continuity, clients, marketing, profit intent. Approval means clients pay
your full invoice; without it they must withhold 30-45 percent. The
[dedicated page on F-skatt](/blog/f-skatt-apply-enskild-firma/) covers how
to describe the business so the first decision is the right one.

## 3. Inkomstdeklaration 1 (SKV 2161)

The annual income tax return for individuals and sole traders. The 2026
deadline is **4 May** (1 June with anstånd). Everything about the form,
e-filing routes, and common mistakes is in
[SKV 2161 explained](/blog/skv-2161-inkomstdeklaration-1-explained/).

## 4. NE-bilaga

The attachment where the enskild firma's business year is declared: revenue,
expenses, result. It rides along with Inkomstdeklaration 1 and is the form
a solo founder actually spends time on. Its logic, and the connection to
your bookkeeping, is on the
[NE-bilaga page](/blog/ne-bilaga-explained/).

## 5. Moms returns (no fixed number; e-service based)

Moms reporting is digital: the return is generated in Skatteverket's
e-service from your registered reporting period, quarterly for most small
firms, due on the 12th of the second month after each quarter. Rates,
thresholds, and the digital-products wrinkle are covered on the
[moms page](/blog/moms-vat-small-business-sweden/).

## 6. What you will NOT need as a one-person firm

- **Inkomstdeklaration 2** (aktiebolag and other legal entities). Different
  return, different deadlines, not yours while you are an enskild firma.
- **Annual report (årsredovisning)**: an AB obligation; an enskild firma has
  no public annual report, only the bookkeeping duty.
- **Payroll forms** (arbetsgivardeklaration): only when you hire someone.

## The pattern behind the list

Swedish small-business paperwork compresses to three recurring motions:
register once (verksamt.se), keep tagged books all year (so the NE-bilaga
is a summation, not an archaeology project), and file on the May cycle. The
[main guide on one-person bookkeeping](/blog/enskild-firma-accounting-sweden/)
shows the shape that makes the third part small, and
[tax deadlines 2026](/blog/swedish-tax-deadlines-2026/) gives you the
calendar in one table.
