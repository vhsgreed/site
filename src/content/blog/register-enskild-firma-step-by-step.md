An enskild firma (sole trader) is the simplest business structure in Sweden:
one owner, no start-up capital requirement, and no separate legal entity.
Registration is a two-track process, one track voluntary, one mandatory.
This page follows the rules as published by
[Bolagsverket](https://bolagsverket.se/en/foretag/enskildnaringsverksamhet/startaenskildnaringsverksamhet.821.html).

## Who can register

You need a **Swedish personal identity number (personnummer) or a
co-ordination number (samordningsnummer)** when registering as a sole
trader in Sweden. Without either, see
[starting a business in Sweden as a foreign citizen](/blog/starting-business-sweden-foreign-citizen/).

## Track 1: Bolagsverket registration (normally voluntary)

Registering with Bolagsverket is voluntary for most sole traders, with
defined exceptions listed on Bolagsverket's "Obligation to register" page.
What voluntary registration buys:

- **Business name protection in the county of registration**: the registered
  name cannot be used by another business registered in the same county.
- A certificate of registration.

The steps:

1. **Apply using form "Registration as a sole trader", number 903 e**, or
   apply online at verksamt.se if you have a Swedish electronic
   identification. The e-service is in Swedish only.
2. **Submit additional details if Bolagsverket asks.** Questions and replies
   are handled through email via verksamt.se.
3. **Bolagsverket registers the business** after approving the proposed
   business name. The processing time is normally about one to two weeks,
   and a certificate of registration is sent in Swedish.

Practical warning from Bolagsverket: do not order a domain name or printed
material before the business name has been approved and registered.

## Track 2: Skatteverket registration (mandatory)

Every sole trader must register with the Swedish Tax Agency. In the online
route, this happens in the same application: when registering online, an
**F-tax card** application and **VAT accounting registration** can be
submitted to Skatteverket at the same time. F-tax is what lets clients pay
without deducting tax, and it is the registration clients will ask for; see
[how to apply for F-skatt](/blog/f-skatt-apply-enskild-firma/). VAT applies
above the turnover threshold; see
[VAT for small businesses in Sweden](/blog/moms-vat-small-business-sweden/).

## What registration costs

There is no start-up capital requirement. Bolagsverket charges fees only for
specific registrations, such as registering the business name; check the
current fee list on Bolagsverket's sole trader fees page before applying.

## After registration

The business result is declared inside the personal income return, with the
NE-bilaga attached to Inkomstdeklaration 1. The full chain of forms and
deadlines is covered in
[the Swedish small business forms guide](/blog/swedish-small-business-forms/)
and [the 2026 tax deadlines](/blog/swedish-tax-deadlines-2026/).
